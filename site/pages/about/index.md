# About

**Café Security Research** publishes open, reproducible research on AI and cybersecurity.<!--if-named: It is run by **{name}**, who also makes the *AI x Cybersecurity* explainer video series.-->

Each study publishes its raw findings and its method, not just its conclusions. Reports, videos and tools are made from the same numbered claims, so a correction reaches all of them.

Alongside the studies are practical guides, such as the [MCP Security Guide](../mcp/index.md), which keep their own repositories and list the source behind every factual claim.

## Using this work

Text and data are licensed under [CC BY 4.0](licence.md) and the scripts under MIT. Please cite the study or guide you use; each study page gives a citation, and the repository has a [`CITATION.cff`](repo:CITATION.cff) file.

## Getting in touch

- To challenge a claim, add evidence or report a mistake, see [contributing](contributing.md).
- Anything that could help an attacker goes through the [private route](security.md) instead.
