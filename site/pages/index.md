## How we work

- **Studies are evidence bases.** Every finding is a numbered claim with a status and its evidence, which you can search in the [claims explorer](claims.md) and re-check yourself.
- **Guides are practical.** They turn what is known into steps you can follow, and list the source behind every factual claim.
- **Both are open to scrutiny.** The text is free to reuse, corrections are made in public, and anyone can [challenge a claim or report a mistake](about/contributing.md).
- **Defensive.** We publish what defenders need and leave out what would mainly help attackers ([publication policy](about/publication-policy.md)).
